Grammar — Present Simple: Wh- Questions
Cấu trúc câu hỏi với What, Who, Where, When, Why, How
📐
Word Order — Trật tự từ trong wh- question
wh-word + do/does + subject + infinitive
▼

We use wh- questions with Present Simple to ask about things, people, places, times, reasons and manner.

Dùng câu hỏi wh- trong Present Simple để hỏi về thứ, người, nơi chốn, thời gian, lý do và cách thức.

📌 Quy tắc do/does
Dùng does khi chủ ngữ là he/she/it (số ít thứ 3) → Where does she live?
Dùng do với I/you/we/they → What do you do?

Wh- words — Khi nào dùng từ nào?
What · Who · Where · When · Why · How · What time
▼
Wh- wordHỏi vềVí dụ
Whatthứ gì / điều gìWhat do you talk about? → About family, life.
What timemấy giờWhat time do you get home? → At seven.
Whongười nàoWho do you call? → My sister or a friend.
Whereở đâuWhere does she live? → Very near me.
Whenkhi nàoWhen do you visit your grandmother? → In the afternoon.
Whytại saoWhy do you drink hot water with lemon? → Because it's good for you.
Hownhư thế nào / cách nàoHow do you feel after that? → Really relaxed.
📌 Who … with? / Who … for?
We often ask: Who do you go with? → With some friends.  |  Who do you work for? → For a big computer company.
📊
Frequency Adverbs trong wh- questions
usually · sometimes · often · never · always
▼

Adverbs of frequency go after the auxiliary verb in questions and before the main verb in statements.

Trạng từ tần suất đứng sau động từ phụ trong câu hỏi và trước động từ chính trong câu khẳng định.
Câu hỏiCâu trả lời
Where do you usually have lunch?I usually have lunch in the park.
Why does Elena sometimes leave work early?She sometimes visits her grandmother.
How often do you clean your kitchen?I always clean it on Sundays.
